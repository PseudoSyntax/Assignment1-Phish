STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics

What Is STEM?

STEM is an interdisciplinary approach to learning and problem-solving that integrates science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. Together, these fields help explain how the world works and how new solutions are created.

Science

Science focuses on understanding the natural world through observation, experimentation, and analysis. It includes disciplines such as biology, chemistry, physics, and environmental science.

Technology

Technology involves the use of tools, systems, and software to solve problems and improve efficiency. It ranges from computer programming and data analysis to digital communication and automation.

Engineering

Engineering applies scientific and mathematical principles to design, build, and improve structures, machines, and processes. Engineers work in areas such as civil, mechanical, electrical, and software engineering.

Mathematics

Mathematics provides the foundation for logical reasoning, measurement, and modeling. It supports all STEM fields through concepts like algebra, geometry, calculus, and statistics.

Why STEM Matters

  • Encourages critical thinking and problem-solving
  • Supports innovation and technological advancement
  • Helps address global challenges
  • Builds skills applicable across many careers
